Prompter

Teleprompter with video recording for iPhone

Paste your script, tap record, and read while the text scrolls right under the front camera lens. The video is saved straight to your Photos library, with no rendering step and no watermark. The app is completely free: no purchases, no subscriptions, no accounts, no ads, no analytics. It makes no network requests, so your scripts and videos never leave your device.

iPhone only, iOS 17 or later. Listed in the App Store as “Teleprompter Prompter”; the icon label reads “Prompter”.

How to use

  1. Paste or type your script (“Paste” drops in whatever is on your clipboard; “Clear” empties the field).
  2. Tap “Record”.
  3. Allow access to the camera and microphone.
  4. Press the red button and start reading. Tap the text to pause. If a take doesn’t work out, tap “Another take”. “Back to script” takes you back to editing.
  5. When you stop, the video is saved to Photos and you’ll see “Saved to Photos”.

FAQ

Does it record audio from my headphones?
Yes. If Bluetooth or wired headphones with a microphone are connected, iOS records the sound from them. To use the iPhone’s own microphone, disconnect the headphones.

Why isn’t the video mirrored?
The preview shows you as in a mirror, but the saved file is not flipped, so it looks the way your viewers will see it.

How do I change the speed and font size?
In the settings on the recording screen: sliders for scroll “Speed” and font size, plus a “Countdown” before the recording starts.

Where are my videos?
In the Photos app on your iPhone. Recordings are saved there automatically.

What happens if I get a phone call while recording?
Recording stops, whatever was captured is saved to Photos, and the app shows a banner telling you so.

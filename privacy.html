2. About the app

Prompter shows your script over the camera view and records video on your iPhone.

3. What data the app collects

None. The app has no server side and makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, ads, trackers or third-party SDKs. No account is needed.

4. What is stored on your device

  • Your script text and settings (speed, font size, countdown) are kept in the app’s local storage.
  • Recorded videos are saved to the system Photos app and belong to you.
  • If you delete the app, the script text and settings are removed; the videos in Photos stay.

5. Permissions and why the app asks for them

  • Camera — to shoot video.
  • Microphone — to record sound with the video.
  • Photos (add only) — to save the finished video. The app does not read your photo library.

6. Sharing with third parties

I don’t share anything with third parties; there is nothing to share. Apple may receive standard diagnostics through iOS if you have allowed that in your phone’s settings, and app ratings go through the App Store’s own system mechanism.

7. Children

The app is not aimed specifically at children and does not collect data about anyone.
